## ARCH-DEC-03 — Dispatch design precedence

**Decided, verbatim:**

> Für das Dispatch-/Failover-Design hat **CR-B01 Vorrang vor widersprechenden oder weniger restriktiven Aussagen aus DET-07**. Kein automatischer Takeover, solange der bisherige Writer noch senden könnte oder Submission-Ergebnisse ungeklärt sind. Ein Zeitablauf/Lease-Timeout allein ist kein Nachweis sicherer Übernahme. Dies ist **Design-Vorrang**, kein implementiertes Fencing und kein nachgewiesenes IBKR-Verhalten.

**Sources:** `P0-Resolution-Contracts-CR-B01-bis-CR-B04-v1.0.md`, CR-B01 (`## 1.1` through `## 1.3`, including P0-B01-D01 and items 6 through 8 of `## 1.2`), has precedence over conflicting or less restrictive statements in `DET-03-bis-DET-08-Architecture-Contracts-v1.0.md`, DET-07 (including DET-07-D01, DET-07-D03, `### Kritische Dispatch-Sequenz`, and `**Grenze des Epoch-Fencing:**`).

**Design precedence rule:** where DET-07 and CR-B01 disagree, or where DET-07 is less restrictive, CR-B01 is the design reading. In particular:

- No automatic takeover while the previous writer might still be able to send, or while submission results are unresolved.
- Elapsed time or a lease timeout alone is not proof of a safe takeover.
- An in-flight send, or an unknown submission result, stays blocked. That block is a design invariant in this record. It is not a runtime configuration and not an implemented fence.
- A database lease does not retract an in-flight socket write. This sentence records the shared design statement already present in both sources. It does not add a protocol.

**Marked `RESOLVED_BY_ARCH_DEC_03`:** only the question of which contract reading has design precedence.

**Still OPEN, and not decided here:** actual broker-session exclusivity, the lease and heartbeat protocol, the dispatch-scope boundary, in-flight send evidence, recovery tests, and IBKR behavior. No default TTL, no automatic resume, no time-based failover, and no new dispatch state transition is introduced. This precedence is not technical verification and not demonstrated IBKR behavior.

XC-04 as a whole stays OPEN. Only that precedence sub-finding is `RESOLVED_BY_ARCH_DEC_03`.
