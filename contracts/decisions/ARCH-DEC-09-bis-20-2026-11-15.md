## Cross-decision consistency

These readings bind the twelve choices together. They do not add a thirteenth choice.

- **OMSQ-05 B overrides any appearance of automatic economic booking in OMSQ-08 A.** Alternative A of OMSQ-08, as written in the proposal, mentions a single consistent identity match that OMSQ-05 has allowed. OMSQ-05 B does not allow that match to post. Identical-payload replay may return a stored result idempotently. It does not create a new economic effect. No automatic economic posting is approved.
- **OMSQ-04 A does not authorize risk release.** OMSQ-06 A leaves the economic-finality predicate undefined. An evidence requirement is not satisfaction of that requirement. No reservation release is enabled.
- **OMSQ-01 A and OMSQ-02 C.** A closure is an immutable historical fact. It is not broker terminality and it is not economic finality. Independent finality slots are a modeling strategy. They are not approved runtime fields. Later corrections belong outside the closure record.
- **OMSQ-03 A, OMSQ-09 A, and ARCH-DEC-03.** A timeout, an empty snapshot, an expired lease, a broker label, or a restart is not proof of non-send, not proof of broker terminality, and not proof of safe failover. Never auto-resubmit. Never auto-takeover.
- **OMSQ-07 A is an acceptance bar, not a matrix ratification.** `XC-03-MATRIX` remains OPEN. No current matrix version is approved. No transition edge is approved.
- **OMSQ-08 A does not choose roles, a reset policy, a two-person exception, or halt-version CAS.** If authorization evidence is absent, the conflict stays quarantined. No adjudicator role and no write path are approved.
- **OMSQ-10 A and OMSQ-11 A.** A transaction and audit principle may be binding while the lock order and the actual audit table stay unresolved. No database implementation is authorized.
- **OMSQ-12 A does not authorize cancel during halt or a risk-reducing action.** `XC-10-ACTION` remains OPEN. No broker dispatch and no action gate are authorized.
