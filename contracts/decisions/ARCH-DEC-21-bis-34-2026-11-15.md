## Cross-decision consistency

These readings bind the fourteen choices together. They do not add a fifteenth choice. They do not supersede ARCH-DEC-13 or ARCH-DEC-14.

- **RISKQ-01 B is conjunction shape only.** The logical conjunction applies to hard-deny composition across the six named scopes. One hard deny blocks. A soft allow never overrides a hard deny. This is not a universal mathematical aggregation and not a numeric approval predicate. No metric catalogue, no numeric algebra, no netting, margin, or stress rule, and no executable budget check is adopted.
- **RISKQ-02 A is an identity and atomicity design requirement.** It is not permission to create a reservation and not permission to commit a partial scope effect. No table, lock order, isolation level, or writer is adopted.
- **RISKQ-04 A, RISKQ-05 A, and RISKQ-06 A together prohibit converting, shrinking, or releasing a hold** after a fill, after a cancel observation, and after a unique broker match. ARCH-DEC-13 quarantines every proposed economic effect, including a uniquely matched fill. The original closure stays immutable.
- **RISKQ-08 A and RISKQ-09 A define no economic-finality predicate.** They cannot be read as permission to release when evidence arrives. The 21-row coverage draft stays unratified. Checklist B from the OMS proposal stays unadopted. Reservation release stays forbidden under ARCH-DEC-14.
- **RISKQ-10 B names seven citation obligations only.** Instrument-policy version, market snapshot, FX snapshot, pricing method, rounding-policy version, base currency, and as-of time are future references a valuation evidence record should be able to cite. They are not a canonical valuation schema, not a numeric policy, not an FX rate, not a rounding mode, not a quantum, and not a freshness number.
- **RISKQ-11 B requires future tests and adopts no protocol.** A later proposed protocol must demonstrate multi-scope budget contention, order-version compare-and-swap, crash before and after commit, and conflict or retry behavior through actual tests. Those tests are `NOT_EXECUTED`. This record does not claim that existing tests prove serializability or deadlock freedom. The ignored PostgreSQL connectivity test is not a pass. No lock order and no isolation level is adopted. Retry stays local only and never resends to the broker.
- **RISKQ-12 A repeats ARCH-DEC-19.** A future reservation mutation requires a distinct authoritative audit record in the same local transaction. If that record cannot be written, the mutation fails. An outbox or export is not the authority. Reconciliation evidence is retained while booking is blocked. No table name is adopted. `XC-06` stays OPEN.
- **RISKQ-13 A grants no adjudication or posting authority.** It cannot supersede ARCH-DEC-13. Manual review alone never posts. No role, writer, workflow, or simulated second approver is introduced. If a future two-person rule requires a second person and none is available, deny.
- **RISKQ-14 B pins future immutable policy lineage.** A future risk-policy change requires a new immutable version id. Historical evaluations keep the version they cited. Required future negative, property, crash, and replay tests stay `NOT_EXECUTED` until they are genuinely run. Recovery neither releases risk nor resubmits a broker order. Identical-payload replay returns the stored result only. No version migration, no new schema, and no broader replay authorization is approved.
